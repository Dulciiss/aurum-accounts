# Privacy Policy for Aurum Accounts

Last updated: October 2026

**Aurum Accounts** ("we", "our", or "the application") is committed to protecting user privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how data is handled within the application.

---

## 1. Local Data Storage & Encryption

- **Zero Cloud Storage:** Aurum Accounts operates locally on your machine. We do not own, maintain, or transmit your data to any external servers or databases.
- **Secure Credential Storage:** Sensitive information, such as account credentials, is stored securely on your local system using OS-level encrypted storage mechanisms (such as Electron Safe Storage / Node Keytar).
- **Application Settings:** Your preferences, tags, and local display options are stored locally on your device.

---

## 2. Information Collection & Analytics

- **No Telemetry or Tracking:** Aurum Accounts does not collect, track, or share any personal data, analytics, usage statistics, or behavioral metrics.
- **Diagnostics:** The "Copy Diagnostics" feature in Settings generates a local Markdown summary of application status and environment details. This tool is triggered solely by the user and explicitly strips out sensitive data, credentials, and user paths before copying to the clipboard.

---

## 3. Third-Party Services & Integration

- **Riot Games / League of Legends Client:** Aurum Accounts interacts locally with the Riot Client running on your machine to detect active session states and display account info. No credentials or session tokens are transmitted anywhere outside your local system.
- **GitHub Releases & Updates:** If you check for updates, the application queries GitHub's public API to compare version tags. No personal data is sent during this process.

---

## 4. Open Source Transparency

Aurum Accounts is an open-source project. All source code responsible for data storage, local client integration, and UI rendering is publicly available for review in our GitHub repository.
